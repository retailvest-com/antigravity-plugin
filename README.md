# RetailVest Smart Money for Antigravity

[![M8ven Score](https://m8ven.ai/badge/mcp/retailvest-com-antigravity-plugin-tcutun?v=616eaa3597931e0a2c0e68ed467f1820&variant=verified)](https://m8ven.ai/mcp/retailvest-com-antigravity-plugin-tcutun?s=readme)

Ask Antigravity what famous investors own and trade, straight from public SEC and CFTC filings.

- **Investor portfolios:** the latest 13F of about 250 well-known investors and hedge funds (Berkshire Hathaway,
  Pershing Square, Scion...), with last quarter's new, added, trimmed and sold positions.
- **Who owns a stock:** which famous funds hold it, how much, and who bought or sold.
- **Copy an investor:** what $10,000 copying an investor's 13F the day it became public would have earned against the
  S&P 500 since 2016.
- **Company briefs:** insider trades (Form 4), audited figures, auditor and largest holders of any US public company.
- **5%+ stakes:** activist 13D and passive 13G filings.
- **Futures positioning:** how speculators are positioned in gold, oil, corn, bonds and other markets (CFTC Commitments
  of Traders), and how crowded each market is.

## Install

```
agy plugin install https://github.com/retailvest-com/antigravity-plugin
```

The first time a tool runs, Antigravity opens RetailVest's sign-in page. Sign in or create a free account and choose
Allow; then paste the code back into Antigravity. A free account includes a monthly allowance of look-ups.

## Try

- "What did Warren Buffett buy and sell last quarter?"
- "Which hedge funds own Nvidia, and who added to it?"
- "Would copying Bill Ackman's 13F have beaten the S&P 500?"
- "Show recent insider trades at Apple."
- "How crowded are speculators in gold futures?"

## Notes

13F filings are quarterly and arrive up to 45 days after the quarter; they show long US positions only. Data and
research, not investment advice.
